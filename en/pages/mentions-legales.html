What we do

PIC Assistance provides remote administrative assistance to self-employed professionals: quotes, invoices (including via Peppol), payment reminders, sorting supporting documents sent on to the client’s accountant, managing emails, calendars and appointments, returning missed calls and online booking.

PIC Assistance does not do bookkeeping and does not prepare or file VAT or tax returns: these tasks are reserved for professionals certified by the ITAA. We work through delegated access to the client’s accounts; clients never share their passwords with us.
