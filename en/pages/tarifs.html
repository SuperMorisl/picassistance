FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you do my accounting or VAT returns?

No. PIC Assistance does not do accounting, VAT returns or tax returns: in Belgium, these tasks are reserved for ITAA-certified accountants. We prepare and sort your documents, then send them to your own accountant.

Do I need to give you my passwords?

Never. We work in your accounts through delegated access: you never share your passwords with us.

Is my data protected?

Yes. We work confidentially and sign a GDPR-compliant data processing agreement with every client.

What is Peppol, and does it apply to me?

Peppol is the network for exchanging electronic invoices. Since 1 January 2026, VAT-registered Belgian businesses must send and receive their B2B invoices electronically via Peppol. We help you set it up and manage it for you, from the Essentiel plan.

Is my admin handled by AI?

No, it’s a human team supported by tools and AI. AI prepares, a human checks and approves.

How does the free trial work?

You try PIC Assistance for 2 weeks, free and with no commitment. Contact us to get started.

Which languages do you work in?

French, Dutch and English.

Which trades and professions?

For self-employed professionals in Belgium: plumbers, electricians, physiotherapists, therapists, consultants, tradespeople… If admin is eating into your time, let’s talk.
