Transparency

What we do, and what we don’t

We do

  • Prepare and send your quotes and invoices, including via Peppol
  • Send reminders for unpaid invoices
  • Sort your receipts and send them to your accountant
  • Manage your inbox, calendar and appointments (depending on the plan)
  • Work through delegated access: you never share your passwords

We don’t do

  • Your accounting
  • Your VAT returns
  • Your tax returns

In Belgium, these tasks are reserved for ITAA-certified accountants. We prepare and sort your documents, then pass them on to your own accountant.
